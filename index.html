Non-Functional Requirements

Reliability: No data is lost or corrupted during processing. Error handling prevents crashes. All AI model outputs are validated before display.

Performance: All major operations completed within 5 seconds. ML models process datasets of 100,000+ records seamlessly. Dashboard loads in under 3 seconds.

Usability: Clean, non-technical UI built for law enforcement officers and government decision-makers across all devices.

Security: JWT-based authentication, strict RBAC, and HTTPS encryption across all endpoints.
